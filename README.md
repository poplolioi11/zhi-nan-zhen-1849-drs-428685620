《指南針》為胡德邁（Thomas Hall Hudson）所著，道光二十九年（1849）寧波福音殿刊本。卷首〈序〉以人身在大海之中而不知向往為喻，說明題名取指南針引導人免於飄蕩之意，並將指南與經歷險危、勸人聽從此書相連；序末言作者不辭遠遊而報「斯佳音」，以此集遍告天下舟人，要讀者保守其魂、依靠耶穌。正文〈奉勸舟人〉從「且夫舟人之事事亦甚危矣」「大水茫茫」說起，接述舟行於波瀾之海的險危，書末以「故梓是書以奉勸」收束，請讀者重視其勸言，終題「指南針終」。

本資料集以哈佛燕京藏本 DRS 428685620 為底本，共取 18 幅固定影像，其中 12 幅含正文，提供 JSON、TXT、TEI 和逐項覆核紀錄。此版為 AI 校讀稿，166 項比對中保留 62 項未解。人工全文二校待完成。

Zhi nan zhen (指南針, The Compass), by Thomas Hall Hudson (胡德邁), was printed by the Fuyin Dian (福音殿) in Ningbo in the 29th year of the Daoguang reign (1849). Its preface explains the title through the image of people adrift on a great sea with no sense of direction, whom a compass can guide so they are no longer tossed about; it ties this guidance to passing through danger and to heeding the book, and closes with the author's resolve to travel far to bring "these good tidings" (斯佳音) to sailors everywhere, urging readers to guard their souls and rely on Jesus. The main text, "Fengquan zhouren" (奉勸舟人, An Exhortation to Boatmen), opens on the perils of a seafarer's life amid "vast waters" (大水茫茫) and the dangers of the waves, and the book ends by stating that it was printed as an exhortation (故梓是書以奉勸), asking readers to take its counsel to heart before the closing line "指南針終".

The catalogue identifies Thomas Hall Hudson (胡德邁) as the author of the historical work. This dataset uses 18 fixed images, including 12 text-bearing pages, from Harvard-Yenching Library DRS 428685620. The AI-reviewed JSON, TXT and TEI transcription retains 62 unresolved comparison items. Full human collation is outstanding.
